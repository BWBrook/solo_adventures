# Ghost Tower of the Witchlight Fens - Narrative-Only Cut

This version keeps the branching adventure, story hooks, named characters, places, choices, traps, and scenario outcomes. It removes the original game's statistics, tactical maps, monster stat blocks, character mechanics, dice thresholds, combat rules, images, credits, and system-specific advice.

Use the numbered entries as a solo narrative frame. When an entry asks whether an attempt succeeds, resolve it with your own system, choose the outcome that fits the fiction, or let the story momentum decide.

## Setup

After the goblin raid and the discovery of the stolen case, you tried to follow the trail alone. You found courage, perhaps even the goblins' lair, but you also learned that pressing on without allies would end badly. You turned back toward Fallcrest, hoping to recruit companions before returning to the deeper mystery of Traevus, the case, and the red-cloaked rider.

Rain ruins your path. The road to Fallcrest is lost in mud, darkness, and unfamiliar trees. While making camp, you hear a cry in the night.
